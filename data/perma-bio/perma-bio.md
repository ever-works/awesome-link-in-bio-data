## Overview

Perma.bio is a closed-source link-in-bio SaaS with flat annual pricing. It offers an Evergreen plan at $18/year and a Pro plan at $39/year, both with 0% platform fees.

## Pricing Model

Paid subscription with two tiers: Evergreen ($18/yr) and Pro ($39/yr). No platform fees on transactions.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS.
