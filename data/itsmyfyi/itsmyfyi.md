## Overview

itsmy.fyi is an open-source alternative to Linktree that enables users to take control of their online presence by creating a personalized link-in-bio page hosted on their own infrastructure.

## Features

- Self-hosted link-in-bio page
- Open-source codebase
- TypeScript implementation
- Privacy-focused alternative to commercial link-in-bio services

## Deployment

Self-hosted.
